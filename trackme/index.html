Track ME: Bill & Pay Tracker

A simple bill and payment tracker for Indian households. Never miss a due date again.

  • See every bill in one place: electricity, gas, broadband, mobile postpaid, insurance premiums, credit cards, EMIs and subscriptions.
  • Get reminded before the due date, on the due date, and the day after if a bill is still unpaid.
  • Optionally find bills automatically from bank and biller SMS, or from bill emails in Gmail.
  • Share any bill SMS or email to Track ME to add it in one tap.
  • Pay in your own UPI app. Track ME never handles your money.

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. SMS and Gmail access are optional and can be turned off at any time. Read the full privacy policy.

How Track ME uses Gmail

If you choose to connect Gmail, Track ME requests read-only access and, only when you tap Scan, searches for bill-like emails from the last 4 months to find amounts and due dates. Emails are processed on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. Track ME's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
