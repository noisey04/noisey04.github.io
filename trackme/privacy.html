Track ME: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Track ME ("the app") is published by Mayank Enterprises ("we"). This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and what happens to it.

Summary

  • Track ME is a bill and payment tracker. It reminds you before bills are due.
  • Everything stays on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data, we have no user accounts, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Reading SMS and Gmail is optional. The app works fully with manual entry.
  • Track ME never moves money. Payments happen in your own UPI app.

What the app accesses and why

SMS (optional, only if you turn it on)

  • What: SMS from registered business senders only (bank and biller sender IDs such as "VM-BSESRP" or "AX-HDFCBK"). Messages from personal phone numbers are never read.
  • Why: To find bills (biller, amount, due date, consumer number) and to recognise bank debit messages, so a bill can be marked paid automatically.
  • How: Messages are read and processed on your phone. The message text is not stored. Only the extracted bill details are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Sharing: SMS data is never uploaded, shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Control: Turn it off anytime in Profile → Bill SMS, or remove the permission in Android Settings.

Gmail (optional, only if you connect it)

  • What: Read-only access (gmail.readonly). The app searches only for bill-like emails from the last 4 months (subject words such as bill, invoice, premium, renewal, statement, payment due) and skips Promotions and Social.
  • When: Only when you tap Connect or Scan.
  • Why: To find bill amounts and due dates.
  • How: Emails are downloaded from Google directly to your phone, processed there, and discarded. Only the extracted bill details are saved. The access token is kept in memory and is not stored.
  • Sharing: Gmail data is never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, never sold, and never read by humans.
  • Control: Disconnect anytime in Profile → Gmail (this revokes access with Google), or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API disclosure: Track ME's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Notifications

Used only for bill reminders and "new bill found" or "marked paid" alerts.

Installed UPI apps

The app checks whether a few UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay) are installed, so it can open the one you choose when you tap Pay. It does not read any data from those apps.

What we store

Bill name, category, amount, due date, repeat cycle, consumer or policy number, your chosen UPI app, and a history of payments you marked or that were matched. All of it is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is excluded from device backups.

Data retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. Profile → Delete all my data erases every bill, payment and setting and revokes Gmail access. Uninstalling the app also removes all its data.

Children

Track ME is not directed to children under 18.

Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date if anything changes. If we ever start collecting data off the device, we will ask for your consent first.
